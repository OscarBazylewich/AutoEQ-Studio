ONLINE DATABASES: WHICH ONES, AND WHY
=====================================
Update (27 September 2026): all 155 databases in the lists are read now (about 54,500 curves). Fixed: 9 sites whose
config has the template's commented-out `// PHONE_BOOK = "phone_book.json"` line (it was read as set: Aquaphobic,
banzai1122, CSI-ZONE, HarutoHiroki, OB Odio, Soundignity, therollo9 IEMs and headphones, zerostresslevel); DHRME
headphones (its page moved to dhrme.nl with a meta refresh); sites on modernGraphTool, a newer graph tool (Doltonius,
SilicaGel IEMs, headphones and earbuds: measurements in data/phones/, targets in data/target(s)/, per-model sample
counts and sets of seatings); Hana (its graph tool keeps measurements in data/measurements/). A download test of 894
random entries from every database: all download except 10 whose files the sites never uploaded (their own graph
pages can't show them either). Hangout.Audio's page now also loads an AES decryption library (its data is kept for its
own page), on top of refusing its files to other programs: still list only; the app doesn't try to get round it.

Update (25 September 2026): every database in squig.link's list is now included and on by default (about
51,000 curves from 154 databases); the analysis below, of which ones are largest and add the most, is kept as a
description of them. Any database can still be turned off in Settings > Sources.

Checked 23 September 2026 (counts taken 21:34 UTC).

WHERE THE LIST CAME FROM
squig.link's "More squiglinks" dropdown (https://squig.link/, loaded from https://squig.link/squigsites.json):
123 reviewers with 148 databases (115 IEM databases on 711-type couplers, 4 on the B&K 5128, 25 headphone
databases, 3 earbud databases). It includes Crinacle's Hangout.Audio (https://graph.hangout.audio). One database
(DHRME headphones) didn't respond (HTTP 404), so its count isn't known; the other 147 were counted.

HOW THEY WERE COUNTED (the same way for every database)
Each database's phone book (<site>/data/phone_book.json, the file squig.link itself reads) was downloaded and:
  models   = unique models: brand + model name, lower case, spaces/punctuation and bracketed notes removed
             (so "Truthear Gate (foam tips)" and "Truthear Gate" count as one model). THE NUMBER USED TO COMPARE.
  entries  = rows in the phone book (one per listed model or variant).
  files    = measurement variants named in the phone book (each one has left/right channel files).
All counts are verified counts from the databases' own lists on the date above, not estimates. Two limits:
  - Names are matched as written, so the same model spelled differently in two databases counts twice: the
    overlap figures below are therefore a little low (the coverage a little high).
  - The AutoEq repository isn't a squig.link site: its count comes from the app's own index of it
    (GitHub's file list): 3,487 unique model names in 6,815 measurement files, across its sources (oratory1990,
    Crinacle's older data, Innerfidelity, Rtings, Headphone.com and others).

HOW THEY WERE CHOSEN
Sizes, largest first, within each kind of rig (IEMs on 711-type couplers; IEMs and headphones on the B&K 5128;
headphones), then how many models each one adds that the larger ones don't already have. A database was
taken when it was above the clear break in sizes in its group:
  - 711 IEMs: 115 databases. The seven with 576 models or more were taken; the next is 492 (a gap of 84 models,
    the largest below 600). Together they cover 3,620 of the 8,350 models found in all 115 (43%); every one of
    them still adds at least 194 models the bigger ones don't have.
  - B&K 5128: only 4 databases; the three with 260 or more were taken (the fourth is tiny). They cover 1,132 of
    1,164 models (97%).
  - Headphones: 25 databases. The three with 259 or more were taken (then 175). They cover 769 of 1,548 (50%).
That is 13 squig.link databases plus the AutoEq repository: more than the rough guide of ten, because the 5128
and headphone groups have few large databases and the 711 group has several of nearly the same size.

KEPT, THOUGH SMALL
  listener800 5128 IEMs and headphones (https://listener800.github.io/): 30 and 88 models. Not in squig.link's

HOW EACH IS INTEGRATED (checked 23 September 2026)
  - squig.link sites: the page's config.js gives the data folder, channels and number of seatings; the phone
    book lists the models; files are "<name> L.txt" / "<name> R.txt" (some only one channel). Indexing and a
    download test succeeded for Aftersound, HBB, Paul Wasabi, Super* Review, Jaytiss, Audio Amigo, Earphones
    Archive (both), Unheard Lab and Gadgetry Tech (6 random models each; a few entries exist for one channel only,
    which the app handles).
  - AutoEq: GitHub's file list and raw.githubusercontent.com downloads, as before.
  - Hangout.Audio (all three): its list (phone_book.json) is public and is indexed, but its server refuses the
    measurement files to anything but its own graph page (HTTP 403 Forbidden). The app doesn't try to get round
    that. Its entries appear in searches marked "open on site ↗" and open on Hangout.Audio (the share link
    squig.link itself uses); "Which measurement to use?" lists them as "also measured by" with links. To use one,
    trace its graph with Trace image.
  Lists refresh every few days; new models in these databases appear by themselves.
