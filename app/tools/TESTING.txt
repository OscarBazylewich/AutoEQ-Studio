TESTING AUTOEQ STUDIO - thanks for trying it!
=============================================

AutoEQ Studio does two things in one window:
- Make an EQ: compares a measurement of your IEMs or headphones with a target curve and finds the filters that make
  one match the other, saved as the file your EQ app needs.
- Devices: gives each playback device on this PC its own EQ, switched by itself (plug in your earphones' dongle and
  their EQ is on). This part works through Equalizer APO (free: https://sourceforge.net/projects/equalizerapo/).
This is a test copy: the point is to find out how it behaves on your PC. Anything odd is worth reporting, even small
things.

GETTING IT RUNNING (about 5 minutes)
  1. Python: if you don't have it, install it from https://www.python.org/downloads/ (the big yellow button;
     keep the default options). Already have it? Skip this; the app says so if yours is too old.
  2. Unzip: right-click the zip > Extract All > Extract. (Not just double-clicking into the zip: the app can't
     run from inside it.) Open the "AutoEQ Studio" folder that comes out.
  3. Double-click "AutoEQ Studio.bat". If Windows asks first ("Windows protected your PC": click "More info",
     then "Run anyway"; or a security warning: click "Run"), that's normal for any script from the internet,
     and it only asks the first time. The first start downloads its packages (about 150 MB, a few minutes, in a
     black window); then the app opens, with an icon by the clock (the tray: click the ^ arrow if it's hidden).
     Later starts are quick.

THINGS TO TRY (tick what you managed; note anything that didn't work or looked wrong)
  Make an EQ
  [ ] It opened on Make an EQ, and the graph shows an orange curve (Truthear GATe) and a dashed target.
  [ ] Press "Guide" (top right) and step through it, watching each step's demo (the pointer). Anything that
      points at the wrong place, overlaps, goes off screen, or is left changed after the tour?
  [ ] Press Run. Note how long it took and the error it shows. Try "All results…" and the graph's View
      (Relative to target) and Y range.
  [ ] Change "Your EQ app" to the one you use, Run again, and Save filters. Is the file in the Saved EQs folder
      ("Open the Saved EQs folder") one your app can load?
  [ ] Find… next to Measurement: search for your own IEMs or headphones (the online databases have thousands),
      Use one and run it.
  [ ] Edit a filter in the result (the table or Sliders): the graph should follow.
  [ ] Listen (under the result), with your earphones in and the volume low: play the music and switch between
      "EQ off" and the EQ (keys 1 and 2). Do you hear the difference? Any clicks, crackles or silence?
  [ ] "✎ Trace image" (by the graph's title): press its "? Guide", then try it on a screenshot of any frequency
      response graph (e.g. from a review site).
  [ ] Help (top right), and F1 while pointing at any setting.
  [ ] Click "Online" (top right) to go offline, and try Find… again: it should say what it can't do offline.

  Devices (only with Equalizer APO installed)
  [ ] Devices: does it list your playback devices, the one playing first? The banner offers to set up Equalizer
      APO for AutoEQ Studio; set it up.
  [ ] Back on Make an EQ, "Use on device" with a result: the EQ should switch on for that device. Play some music:
      do you hear it? Switch it Off in Devices (or from the tray icon's menu) and on again.
  [ ] Devices > your device: does the line under its EQ ("Check again" asks APO anew) match what you hear?
  [ ] Plug in or connect another output (headphones, a dongle, Bluetooth): does the tray say which EQ is on?
  (To undo it all: Settings > “Take AutoEQ Studio out of APO…”.)

  General
  [ ] Resize the window small and large, and try it maximised. Light and dark (Windows' setting) both readable?
  [ ] Close the window (it stays in the tray), then Quit from the tray icon's menu and start it again: your
      choices should still be there.

SENDING FEEDBACK
  Send:
    - what you tried and what happened (or didn't),
    - screenshots of anything that looked wrong (Win+Shift+S takes one),
    - the files app.log and setup.log from the "data" folder (they hold no personal files; your Windows user name
      may appear in a folder path),
    - your Windows version, and how long the runs took.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Everything runs on your PC. It only goes online to download its packages once and to search/download curves;
    Offline (top right) stops even that.
  - Your files are in Documents\AutoEQ Studio (Targets, Measurements, Saved EQs, Presets; Settings > Your files
    opens it or chooses another), the app's settings and logs in this folder's data\. The device EQs are kept in
    Equalizer APO's config folder, in "AutoEQ Studio".
  - To remove it: Settings > “Take AutoEQ Studio out of APO…”, untick Start with Windows (Settings), quit it from
    the tray, delete the folder.
    (If the black window said it put its packages in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AutoEQ Studio, delete that folder too.)
  - README.txt explains every part of the app.
