A TEST OF AUTOEQ STUDIO
=======================

AutoEQ Studio has two parts in one window:
- Make an EQ: it compares a measurement of your IEMs or headphones with a target curve. It finds the filters that
  make the measurement agree with the target. It saves them as the file that your EQ app uses.
- Devices: each playback device on your PC has its EQ, and the EQ changes automatically. When you connect the
  dongle of your IEMs, their EQ is on. This part operates through Equalizer APO (free software:
  https://sourceforge.net/projects/equalizerapo/).
This is a test copy. The test must show how the app operates on your PC. Record each unusual result, also if it is
small.

START THE APP (approximately 5 minutes)
  1. Python: if you do not have Python, install it from https://www.python.org/downloads/ (the large yellow
     button). Do not change the settings of its installer. If you have Python, go to step 2. The app tells you if
     it must have a newer version of Python.
  2. To unzip the zip file, right-click it. Click "Extract All". Then click "Extract". It is not sufficient to
     double-click the zip file, because the app cannot start from the zip file. Open the "AutoEQ Studio" folder
     that you get.
  3. Double-click "AutoEQ Studio.bat". Windows can show a message first. For "Windows protected your PC", click
     "More info". Then click "Run anyway". For a "Security Warning" window, click "Run". These messages are usual
     for a file from the internet, and Windows shows them only one time.
  At the first start, the app downloads its packages in a black window (approximately 150 MB, some minutes). Then
  the app opens, with an icon near the clock (the tray). If you cannot see the icon, click the ^ arrow. After the
  first start, the app starts quickly.

DO THESE TESTS
  Write "x" in the [ ] of each test that you completed. Record each item that did not operate, or that the app
  showed incorrectly.

  Make an EQ
  [ ] The app opened on Make an EQ. The graph shows an orange curve (Truthear GATe) and the target as a dashed
      line.
  [ ] Click Guide (at the top right). Do each step. Look at the demo of each step (the pointer). Does a part of
      the Guide show at an incorrect position, on a different part of the window, or out of the screen? Is an item
      different after the Guide?
  [ ] Click Calculate. Record the time that the app used and the error that it shows. Use "All results…". Above
      the graph, use View (Difference from target) and Y range.
  [ ] In "Your EQ app", select the EQ app that you use. Click Calculate again. Then click Save filters. Can your
      EQ app load the file from the Saved EQs folder ("Open the Saved EQs folder")?
  [ ] Click Find… (the Measurement row). Search for your IEM or headphone (the online databases have thousands of
      measurements). On a result, click Use. Then click Calculate.
  [ ] Edit a filter in Your result (Table or Sliders). The graph must show the change.
  [ ] Listen (below Your result): use your IEM or headphone with a low volume. Click Play to play the music.
      Change the version between "EQ off" and the EQ (keys 1 and 2). Do you hear the difference? Do you hear
      short noises that are not in the music? Does the sound stop?
  [ ] Click "Trace a graph image…" (near the title of the graph). In the tracer, click Guide. Then trace a
      screenshot of a frequency response graph (for example, from a website about IEMs or headphones).
  [ ] Click Help (at the top right). When the pointer is on a setting, press F1.
  [ ] Click Online (at the top right): the app becomes offline. Then use Find… again. Find… must show a message
      that starts with "Offline:".

  Devices (only if Equalizer APO is installed)
  [ ] Open Devices. Does it show your playback devices, with the connected devices first? The bar at the top has
      "Add AutoEQ Studio to APO…". Click it. Then click "Add to APO".
  [ ] On Make an EQ, with a result, click "Use on device". Select a device. The EQ must be on for that device.
      Play music: do you hear the EQ? In Devices (or in the right-click menu of the tray icon), select Off. Then
      select the EQ again.
  [ ] Devices > your device: does the line below its EQ agree with the sound that you hear? ("Examine again" gets
      a new result from Equalizer APO.)
  [ ] Connect a different playback device (headphones, a dongle or a Bluetooth device). Does the tray icon show
      which EQ is on (in a notification or in its tooltip)?
  (To undo all these changes, click Settings > "Remove AutoEQ Studio from APO…".)

  General
  [ ] Make the window small. Then make it large. Then maximize it. In light mode and in dark mode (the Windows
      setting), can you read all the text?
  [ ] Close the window (the app stays in the tray). In the right-click menu of the tray icon, click
      "Stop AutoEQ Studio". Start the app again. It must have your selections.

SEND THIS INFORMATION
  Send these items:
    - The tests that you did, and the result of each test (also if a test had no result).
    - Screenshots of each item that the app showed incorrectly (press Windows + Shift + S to make a screenshot).
    - The files app.log and setup.log from the "data" folder. They contain none of your files. A folder path in
      them can show the name of your Windows account.
    - Your Windows version, and the time that the app used to calculate each EQ.

MORE INFORMATION
  - All the parts of the app operate on your PC. The app connects to the internet only to download its packages
    one time, and to search for curves and download them. If you click Online (at the top right), the app becomes
    offline and does not connect to the internet.
  - Your files are in Documents\AutoEQ Studio (Targets, Measurements, Saved EQs, Presets). Settings > Your files
    opens this folder, or selects a different folder. The settings and the logs of the app are in the "data"
    folder of the "AutoEQ Studio" folder from the zip file. The EQs of your devices are in the config folder of
    Equalizer APO, in "AutoEQ Studio".
  - To remove the app:
      1. If you added AutoEQ Studio to APO, click Settings > "Remove AutoEQ Studio from APO…".
      2. In Settings, clear the Start with Windows tick box.
      3. In the right-click menu of the tray icon, click "Stop AutoEQ Studio".
      4. Delete the "AutoEQ Studio" folder.
      5. If the black window showed that it put the packages in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AutoEQ Studio\venv, also delete
         that folder.
  - README.txt gives more information about each part of the app.
