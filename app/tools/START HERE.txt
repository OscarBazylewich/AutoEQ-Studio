AUTOEQ STUDIO
=============

AutoEQ Studio makes EQs for IEMs and headphones, and gives each playback device on your PC its EQ.
- Make an EQ: it compares a measurement of your IEMs or headphones with a target curve (the sound that you want).
  It finds the filters that make the measurement agree with the target. It saves them as the file that your EQ app
  uses.
- Devices: each playback device has its EQ, and the EQ changes automatically. When you connect the dongle of your
  IEMs, their EQ is on. This part operates through Equalizer APO (free software:
  https://sourceforge.net/projects/equalizerapo/).

START IT
  1. To unzip the zip file, right-click it. Click "Extract All". Then click "Extract". It is not sufficient to
     double-click the zip file, because the app cannot start from the zip file. Put the "AutoEQ Studio" folder
     where it can stay (for example, on the Desktop).
  2. Double-click "AutoEQ Studio.bat". If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info". Then click
     "Run anyway". This message is usual for a program from the internet, and Windows shows it only one time. You
     do not install other items: all the parts that the app uses are in its "runtime" folder.
  3. The app opens on Make an EQ, with a sample IEM and a sample target. To see how it operates, click Calculate.
     Guide (at the top right) shows you each part. Help tells you about each name that the app uses. When the
     pointer is on a setting, press F1: Help opens at that setting.
  When you close the window, the app stays in the tray near the clock. There, it continues to control the EQs of
  your devices. If you cannot see its icon, click the ^ arrow. "Stop AutoEQ Studio" is in the right-click menu of
  the tray icon.

YOUR IEMs OR HEADPHONES
  1. Click Find… (the Measurement row). It searches thousands of measurements in the online databases. Or click
     Add… to add a file from your PC.
  2. In "Your EQ app", select your EQ app.
  3. Click Calculate.
  4. Click Save filters. Or click "Use on device" to put the EQ directly on a device through Equalizer APO.

MORE INFORMATION
  - All your data stays on your PC. The app connects to the internet only to search for curves and to download
    them. If you click Online (at the top right), the app becomes offline and does not connect to the internet.
  - Your files are in Documents\AutoEQ Studio: Targets, Measurements, Saved EQs, Presets. Settings > Your files
    opens this folder, or selects a different folder. The settings of the app are in its "data" folder. Settings >
    Backup saves your files and the settings in one zip file.
  - To remove the app:
      1. If you added AutoEQ Studio to APO, click Settings > "Remove AutoEQ Studio from APO…".
      2. In Settings, clear the Start with Windows tick box.
      3. In the right-click menu of the tray icon, click "Stop AutoEQ Studio".
      4. Delete the "AutoEQ Studio" folder.
  - README.txt gives more information about each part of the app.
