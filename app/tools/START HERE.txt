AUTOEQ STUDIO
=============

AutoEQ Studio makes EQs for headphones and IEMs, and gives each playback device on your PC its own EQ.
- Make an EQ: compares a measurement of your headphones or IEMs with a target curve (the sound you want) and finds
  the filters that make one match the other, saved as the file your EQ app needs.
- Devices: gives each playback device its own EQ, switched by itself (plug in your earphones' dongle and their EQ
  is on). This part works through Equalizer APO (free: https://sourceforge.net/projects/equalizerapo/).

STARTING IT
  1. Unzip: right-click the zip > Extract All > Extract. (Not just double-clicking into the zip: it can't run from
     inside it.) Put the "AutoEQ Studio" folder somewhere it can stay, e.g. Documents.
  2. Double-click "AutoEQ Studio.bat". If Windows asks first ("Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then
     "Run anyway"), that's normal for any program from the internet, and it only asks once. Nothing needs
     installing: everything it uses is in its runtime folder.
  3. It opens on Make an EQ with a sample IEM and target already in: press Run to see what it does. "Guide" (top
     right) walks you through each part, and Help (or F1 on any setting) explains every term.
  Closing the window leaves it running in the tray by the clock (click the ^ arrow if the icon is hidden): that's
  what keeps your devices' EQs switching. Quit is in the tray icon's right-click menu.

YOUR OWN EARPHONES
  Find… next to Measurement searches thousands of measurements online (or Add… a file from your PC). Pick your EQ
  app, Run, then Save filters, or "Use on device" to put the EQ straight onto a device through Equalizer APO.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Everything stays on your PC. It only goes online to search for and download curves; Offline (top right) stops
    even that.
  - Your files are in Documents\AutoEQ Studio: Targets, Measurements, Saved EQs, Presets (Settings > Your files
    opens it or chooses another folder). The app's settings are in its own data\ folder. Settings > Backup puts
    them all in one .zip.
  - To remove it: Settings > "Take AutoEQ Studio out of APO…" (if you set it up), untick Start with Windows, quit
    it from the tray, delete the folder.
  - README.txt explains every part in detail.
