AUTOEQ STUDIO {version}
===================

AutoEQ Studio makes EQs for IEMs and headphones, and gives each playback device on your PC its EQ.
- Make an EQ: it compares a measurement of your IEMs or headphones with a target curve (the sound that you want).
  It finds the filters that make the measurement agree with the target. It saves them as the file that your EQ app
  uses.
- Devices: each playback device has its EQ, and the EQ changes automatically. When you connect the dongle of your
  IEMs, their EQ is on. This part operates through Equalizer APO (free software:
  https://sourceforge.net/projects/equalizerapo/).
AutoEQ Studio operates on Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

INSTALL IT
  1. Double-click "AutoEQ Studio Setup {version}.exe". You can start it directly from the zip file.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info". Then click "Run anyway". Windows shows this
     message for each program that has no digital signature, and only one time.
  3. Complete the steps of Setup. Setup installs the app only for you, without an administrator password. It
     contains all the necessary parts: you do not download other items. If you want a desktop shortcut, select
     "Create a desktop shortcut".
  4. AutoEQ Studio opens on Make an EQ, with a sample IEM and a sample target. To see how it operates, click
     Calculate. Guide (at the top right) shows you each part. Help tells you about each name that the app uses.
     When the pointer is on a setting, press F1: Help opens at that setting.
  When you close the window, the app stays in the tray near the clock. There, it continues to control the EQs of
  your devices. If you cannot see its icon, click the ^ arrow. "Stop AutoEQ Studio" is in the right-click menu of
  the tray icon.

AN EQ FOR EACH DEVICE (optional)
  1. Install Equalizer APO.
  2. In its Configurator, select the devices that must have an EQ.
  3. When the Configurator tells you to restart your PC, restart it.
  4. In AutoEQ Studio, open Devices.
  5. Click "Add AutoEQ Studio to APO…" (the bar at the top). Then click "Add to APO".
  Then each device gets the EQ that you select for it.

YOUR IEMs OR HEADPHONES
  1. Click Find… (the Measurement row). It searches thousands of measurements in the online databases. Or click
     Add… to add a file from your PC.
  2. In "Your EQ app", select your EQ app.
  3. Click Calculate.
  4. Click Save filters. Or click "Use on device" to put the EQ directly on a device through Equalizer APO.

MORE INFORMATION
  - All your data stays on your PC. The app connects to the internet only to search for curves and to download
    them. If you click Online (at the top right), the app becomes offline and does not connect to the internet.
  - Your files are in Documents\AutoEQ Studio: Targets, Measurements, Saved EQs, Presets. Settings > Your files
    opens this folder, or selects a different folder. Settings > Backup saves all your data in one zip file.
  - To update the app, start a newer Setup. Your settings and your files stay.
  - To remove the app, use Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > AutoEQ Studio > Uninstall. Each device gets
    the initial settings of Equalizer APO. Then you select if Uninstall also deletes your settings.
    Documents\AutoEQ Studio always stays.
