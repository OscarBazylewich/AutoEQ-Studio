AUTOEQ STUDIO {version}
==================

AutoEQ Studio makes EQs for headphones and IEMs, and gives each playback device on your PC its own EQ.
- Make an EQ: compares a measurement of your headphones or IEMs with a target curve (the sound you want) and finds
  the filters that make one match the other, saved as the file your EQ app needs.
- Devices: gives each playback device its own EQ, switched by itself (plug in your earphones' dongle and their EQ
  is on). This part works through Equalizer APO (free: https://sourceforge.net/projects/equalizerapo/).
Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

INSTALLING
  1. Double-click "AutoEQ Studio Setup {version}.exe" (you can run it straight from the zip).
  2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway". It says that about any
     program that isn't digitally signed; it only asks once.
  3. Click through Setup. It installs for you only, needs no administrator password, and has everything inside
     (nothing else to download). Tick "Create a desktop shortcut" if you want one.
  4. AutoEQ Studio opens on Make an EQ with a sample IEM and target already in: press Run to see what it does.
     "Guide" (top right) walks you through each part, and Help (or F1 on any setting) explains every term.
  Closing the window leaves it running in the tray by the clock (click the ^ arrow if the icon is hidden): that's
  what keeps your devices' EQs switching. Quit is in the tray icon's right-click menu.

AN EQ FOR EACH DEVICE (optional)
  Install Equalizer APO first; in its Configurator tick the devices you want EQ on, and restart your PC when it
  asks. Then in AutoEQ Studio open Devices and press Set up. Each device then gets the EQ you choose for it.

YOUR OWN EARPHONES
  Find... next to Measurement searches thousands of measurements online (or Add... a file from your PC). Pick your
  EQ app, Run, then Save filters, or "Use on device" to put the EQ straight onto a device through Equalizer APO.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Everything stays on your PC. It only goes online to search for and download curves; Offline (top right) stops
    even that.
  - Your files are in Documents\AutoEQ Studio: Targets, Measurements, Saved EQs, Presets (Settings > Your files
    opens it, or chooses another folder). Settings > Backup puts everything in one .zip.
  - Updating: run a newer Setup; your settings and files stay.
  - Removing it: Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > AutoEQ Studio > Uninstall. It switches your devices
    back to Equalizer APO's own settings and asks whether to delete your settings too; Documents\AutoEQ Studio
    always stays.
